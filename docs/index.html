Call aane ke chances kaise badhaye

  1. Har JD padh ke resume ke Skills/Summary mein uske exact shabd daalo (sirf jo sach mein aata hai).
  2. "Strong" wale pehle karo. "Stretch" wale bhi zaroor bhejo, kyunki experience ki shart aksar flexible hoti hai.
  3. Job post hone ke 48 ghante ke andar apply karo. Foundit pe "first 10 applicants" wali jobs sabse achhi hain.
  4. Big companies (Gartner, Airtel, MakeMyTrip, Paytm) mein apply karne ke baad unke kisi employee se referral maango.
  5. Salary mein ab 30k se kam pe mat jao. Jahan pay likha hai, wahan range ka upar wala hissa maango.
